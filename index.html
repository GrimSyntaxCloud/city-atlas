    02 / BEHIND THE MAP

    Old data.
    New ways to see it.

    This visualization began as a D3 coursework exercise in 2018: a US map, state labels, and circles sized by city population. This edition preserves those elements and adds a searchable city index, rank filters, and keyboard-friendly exploration.

    The original D3 by Example dataset is a historical sample, not current census data. Its values may mix reference years; this demo does not assert a single census year or a current ranking.
